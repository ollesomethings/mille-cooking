# Prototype screens

## Scenario launcher

A once-per-session "This is a demo." disclaimer dialog (`src/components/proto/disclaimer.tsx`, mounted at the root in app/_layout.tsx) overlays whatever screen the session opens on — launcher or preset links — stating nothing is live in the real app, all data is mock, and everything shown is in the works.

Shareable preset link: `/calm` (app/calm.tsx) skips the launcher entirely — seeds the existing-teen scenario with the calming tool set on and lands in CHAT with a dismissible pointer card ("Psst — your space has calming tools now", `src/components/hub-hint-card.tsx`) that scrolls the pager to the Hub (hub_opened{source: chat_hint}). Send this URL to testers who should see the mental-health tools without touching any toggles; reloading mid-session returns to the launcher, reopening the link re-seeds.

The app opens on a prototype-only launcher (`src/components/proto/scenario-picker.tsx`, gated in `app/index.tsx`) — responsive: the content column scrolls, the phone fan renders behind it and only on viewports ≥680px tall, and fan size scales with both axes, so the option toggles are visible or reachable at every screen size. The "Know your mentor" toggle row is currently hidden (feature stays wired via scenario-store.mentorTools; re-add the ToggleRow to restore). Two scenario cards with descriptions (**New teen** 🌱 / **Existing teen** 💬) plus a session-options group with brand toggle switches (`src/components/proto/toggle-switch.tsx`): "🌙 Mentor offline" and "⏭️ Skip onboarding" (new-teen only — `chooseScenario` + immediate `completeOnboarding('John')`, landing in chat already matched, no signup questions). **New teen** runs the FULL onboarding (`app/onboarding.tsx`, structure/copy/components from `mono/link-v2 (auth)+(onboarding)`): welcome → phone → OTP → state → preferred-name → legal name → birthday → zipcode → sex → pronouns → race/ethnicity → expectation-setting pager (4 slides, blueLoops bg) → ideal-vibe → mentors-loading (Moodie) → we-found-match (collage) → mentor detail (hero + vinyl + voice note, "say hi to {name}") → mentor browse ("Your matches" carousel + "Choose for me") — and lands in a fresh account; the chosen mentor (`src/data/mentor-candidates.ts`, real photos from mono assets) drives avatars/greeting/welcome note app-wide. Intro video and the post-match "get to know {mentor}" story flow are NOT built yet. **Existing user** seeds three weeks of history with Noah. Scenario state: `src/stores/scenario-store.ts`; reload to switch.

Status: **stub** (renders, minimal interaction) · **interactive** (full flow works) · **polished** (reviewed on a real phone).

## Offline-hours self-care flow

## Hub = the teen's space (tools + with-mentor)

There is exactly ONE surface where tools live: the Hub pane of the home pager (`src/components/hub-tab.tsx`) — no separate sheet or route. Structure top-to-bottom: header (relationship name "da boyzz", "Since October 2025" — unchanged), Recommended slot (hidden unless `src/lib/recommendation.ts` `getRecommendation()` returns one; set-aware prototype rule: midnight–6am local recommends Calm my body ("It's late. This one helps.") on the default set, Calm cards ("Too wired to sleep? Pull one card.") on the mental-health set; recomputed on every visit and on toggle change; swappable for a real system), Tools 2×2 grid (`src/data/selfcare-tools.ts` — the retired picker's four cards, open to everyone, nothing gated; `/selfcare` redirects home), "With {mentor}" (greyed SOON chips: Swaps, Hot Takes, Notes, Deep Thoughts, Connect — followed by the Hub's original content: welcome carousel, notes, moments, swaps), and ONE pinned crisis link. The + FAB is unchanged. Entry points, all landing on this pane: the "Your space" header pill (chat/corner tabs; hidden on the hub itself), the offline pointer card, and tool exits' "Back to your space" (via `src/stores/nav-store.ts` requestHub — the Hub is a pager pane, not a route). Every tool is ≤2 taps from home. Analytics: hub_opened{source: home_entry|offline_pointer|tool_exit} (request-sourced events fire at request time since the pane may already be fronted), hub_tool_opened{tool, was_recommended}, recommendation_shown/tapped.

Launcher toggle "Know your mentor" (`scenario-store.mentorTools`, mutually exclusive with the mental-health toggle) swaps the Hub's tools for the icebreaker set (`MENTOR_TOOLS`), built from the "Get to know your mentor" whiteboard: `/know/questions` (20 questions — 8 flip cards (rotateY, tap the question and it turns over) with deck pips; the mentor's answers are pre-left with his intro and labeled "ANSWERED WITH NOAH'S INTRO · NOT LIVE"; optional typed answers, sent to chat at the end as ONE message or kept private), `/know/songs` (Our playlist — the mentor started one with his intro: three tracks + his on-repeat spinning on a hand-built vinyl with a droppable tonearm, hold the record to stop it; the teen digs through an 8-track crate, adds three (dashed slots fill with YOU rows), names it (Caveat input + suggestion chips) → hub note with the full tracklist + a chat message), `/know/boards` (Vibe boards — the mentor's 4-image board from his gallery assets; teen pins 3 from a 9-image pool into dashed board slots (spring in, tap a slot to unpin) → hub moment "vibe board, for Noah"), `/know/story` (Make a story — photo + eyebrow chip + headline with live preview → real story entity on the hub, opens in the existing story viewer). Shared exit: `know-finish.tsx` (confetti + Back to your space). Analytics: know_tool_completed{tool,…}, know_answers_sent. No simulated presence anywhere — everything from the mentor is framed as pre-left intro content.

Launcher toggle "Mental health tools" (`scenario-store.mentalHealthTools`) swaps the Hub's tool set for the calming line-up (`CALM_TOOLS` in `src/data/selfcare-tools.ts`): Calm my body (same breathing tool), 5-4-3-2-1 (the grounding hunt, relabeled), `/selfcare/paint` (Paint it calm — pick a subject first: Evening lake / Night sky / Window plant, each ~9 hand-drawn SVG regions with numbered paints; wrong taps are a soft no-op, per-fill burst; finishing springs in a polaroid keepsake ('night sky — sep 21') with 'Keep it in your space' — adds the finished painting to the hub grid as a real moment entity (SVG data-URI image, caption 'painted: …', paint_kept analytics) — or 'Let it go'), `/selfcare/star` (Star breathing, after Dr. Juliet Young's exercise, credited on screen — finger-trace the ten edges of a five-point star, each edge a breath phase (In/Hold/Out) that fills with its color under your finger; finger position projects onto the current edge, forward-only, with a 48px wander tolerance and no punishment; corner haptic ticks, lap success buzz, 2 laps to finish; "Guide me" auto-traces as the tap alternative), and `/selfcare/calmdeck` (Calm cards — shuffled deck of 14 one-line calm-down tips, swipe or tap the top card away, spring-back on short drags, hairline progress; finishing the deck plays a finale: the five card backs spring into a fan with confetti, 'That's the whole deck.' / 'fourteen ways down, whenever you need one.'). Both new tools keep the crisis link and "Stop early"/"I'm done" exits, and report offline_tool_completed (regions_filled / tips_seen).

Launcher toggle "Mentor offline" (`scenario-store.mentorOffline`) works with both scenarios and combines with Skip onboarding (offline + skip = fresh account, holding message, no greeting). Offline chat (`src/components/mentor-offline-block.tsx`): live countdown to 5pm ET ("Noah is back in 9h 24m", `offline.ts mentorReturnCountdown`, offline_countdown_seen), dimmed resting message history, AUTO-MESSAGE holding bubble, and a dismissible pointer card into /space (a heads-up, never a modal/auto-launch). Tool exit (`/selfcare/done`) primary CTA is context-aware: offline → "Remind me when {mentor} is on", online → "Go to your chat"; secondary "Back to your space". Chat then shows `src/components/mentor-offline-block.tsx`: honest availability ("Noah is back at 5pm ET"), the mentor's holding message labeled AUTO-MESSAGE (never a simulated live person — a new-user signup gets NO greeting), the opt-in card ("Having a hard moment right now?" → never auto-launched), and the crisis link. Flow routes: `/selfcare` (picker, 2×2 grid: Calm my body / Get it out / Distract me / Show me I'm not alone) → `/selfcare/breathe` (box breathing 4-4-4-4 as a graffiti piece: phase-colored circle/background via interpolateColor, spray-splat bursts per phase, twin counter-rotating halos, in-circle 4-3-2-1 count with tick haptics, box-progress dots; 2-min default, stop anytime), `/selfcare/vent` (private by default, optional share-with-mentor; desk toolbar: 5 fonts, 5 inks, 4 papers, draggable zine stickers — tinted brand scribbles + handwritten Caveat/Avantt word stickers ('ugh.', 'SO DONE', 'fr fr'…), drop-to-toss trash zone — PanResponder, paper measured by a plain View since LinearGradient/Animated.View don't forward onLayout on web), `/selfcare/ground` (5-4-3-2-1 camera scavenger hunt, camera is local-only and says so; graceful no-camera fallback; "Found one" grabs the current frame in-memory only for ~1s: polaroid pops in then explodes into confetti — shards-only when no camera), `/selfcare/notalone` (read-only anonymous confessions feed: once-per-session intro, theme chips, one full-screen card at a time, breather card every 9, hairline progress, anonymous per-card "me too" resonance (no counters, no names, nothing sent — confession_me_too analytics only) with a WordBurst of rising handwritten agreements ('same', 'fr', 'this') + spray dots in the theme color; per-theme paint colors on label/progress; still no comments/sharing/submissions — data in `src/data/confessions.json`, 58 real anonymized safety-passed submissions, clinical review still required before shipping) → `/selfcare/done` (tie-back + "Remind me when they're on" push stub; not_alone variant adds "Talking to someone who gets it is the next step."). `/resources` mirrors mono's mentor-offline sheet (911/988/Crisis Text Line/Warm Line, tel:/sms: links) and is one tap from chat (shield icon too) and from every self-care screen via `TalkNowLink`. Tool identities are hand-drawn SVG glyphs (`src/components/proto/tool-glyphs.tsx` — wobbly marker strokes, no emoji anywhere in tool chrome); the not-alone intro art is the 'tired' Moodie. Analytics per spec in `src/lib/analytics.ts` (console, hour bucket + 0–6am flag). Haptics via `src/lib/buzz.ts` — real on native, navigator.vibrate on Android web, console elsewhere (`src/web/expo-haptics-stub.ts`). Out of scope: mentor-side visibility, voice vent, streaks; confessions submissions/moderation/reactions (ADT-6).
