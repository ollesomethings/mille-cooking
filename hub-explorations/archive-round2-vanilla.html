Recommendation

Ship concept 2, organized by mode, as the Hub’s skeleton — and steal concept 3’s Keepsakes idea for history. The Hub’s job after the Sep 10 decision is to be a reliable, legible shelf that absorbs an unknown number of future tools with zero routing decisions; the homepage is the only concept where that’s structurally guaranteed and findability holds. Its real weakness — dashboard coldness — is a content-design problem (voice, card art, the For You rail), not a structural one; the player’s and desk’s weaknesses (findability, discovery-at-scale) are structural. The desk is the braver bet and the best rematch story, but it bets the cold start on default-pin curation — a per-teen policy, i.e. the thing we’re designing out. Revisit it when tools are stable. The player shouldn’t die: it’s the right moment surface (waiting-for-match, mentor offline) — a feed you can point into that itself points back at the Hub.
